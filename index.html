The House

He kept the name.

Every family has a son who refuses to follow. Ours went back to Roubaix, broke into his father's shuttered perfume house and filled it with smoke.

What he bottled there was never meant to please: dark, heavy scents that refuse to fade, for men who would rather be remembered than liked.

Wear what you weren't raised to.
